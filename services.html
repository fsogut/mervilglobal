What We Do

Three sectors.

01
Railway & Multimodal Logistics

End-to-end freight across critical corridors.

We specialize in railway and multimodal freight logistics along some of the most strategically significant trade corridors in Eurasia — including the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route (TITR / Middle Corridor) and routes through Turkey, the Caucasus, and Central Asia.

With deep knowledge of regional operators, border procedures, and intermodal connections, MERVIL Global designs reliable solutions that combine rail, road, and maritime legs for optimal transit times and cost efficiency.

  • Railway freight forwarding (block trains & single wagons)
  • Multimodal door-to-door solutions
  • Trans-Caspian / Middle Corridor routing
  • Customs & border clearance coordination
  • Cargo tracking and operational support
02
Container Solutions

Moving and managing containers with precision.

MERVIL Global operates in the buying, selling, and leasing of ISO shipping containers — new and used — across key trade lanes connecting Europe, Turkey, the Caucasus, and Central Asia.

Our approach is grounded in quality assessment, market knowledge, and a commitment to matching the right asset to the right client need. Whether you're looking to acquire a fleet, lease individual units, or offload surplus containers, we provide straightforward, reliable solutions.

  • New & used ISO container sales
  • Short and long-term container leasing
  • Container fleet advisory
  • Depot and positioning coordination
03
Commodity Trading

Transparent sourcing of fertilizers and industrial chemicals.

MERVIL Global actively trades in fertilizer raw materials and chemicals — including key inputs for agricultural and industrial production — connecting suppliers and buyers across the region with transparency and integrity.

We understand the sensitivity and complexity of commodity markets and bring a disciplined, relationship-based approach that prioritizes supply chain reliability and fair dealing above all.

  • Fertilizer raw materials (potash, urea, phosphates)
  • Industrial chemicals sourcing
  • Supplier & buyer matching
  • Contract structuring and documentation
  • Logistics coordination for bulk cargoes
