Our Story

Built on trust.
Driven by expertise.

From regional director
to independent founder.

MERVIL Global was founded by a professional with over a decade of experience as a regional director at an international company, overseeing logistics, trade, and business development across Turkey, the Caucasus, and Central Asia.

After years of navigating complex cross-border supply chains and building lasting relationships with clients and partners throughout the region, MERVIL Global was established as an independent company — purpose-built to deliver premium, relationship-first service in three interconnected sectors.

The name MERVIL carries personal meaning: derived from the first letters of the founder's birth city and current city of residence, it reflects a philosophy of embedding identity and intention into every aspect of business.

Relationship First

We prefer a small circle of trusted partners over aggressive growth. Every engagement is built on mutual respect and long-term commitment.

Premium Service

Quality and reliability are non-negotiable. We hold ourselves to the highest standards in every transaction and communication.

Meaningful Work

We focus on work that matters — to our clients, to the trade corridors we serve, and to the communities along those routes.

Deep Regional Knowledge

Ten years of on-the-ground experience across Turkey, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, and Uzbekistan — this is our home territory.

"We built MERVIL Global not to be the biggest player in the room, but to be the most trusted one — for the clients who value precision, reliability, and a genuine partnership."
— Founder & CEO, MERVIL Global
